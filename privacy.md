# Tab Volume Dial Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Aimplify Solutions LLC publishes Tab Volume Dial, a Chrome extension that adjusts the sound of individual tabs. This policy describes how the extension handles user data.

## Tab audio

When you apply an audio adjustment, Tab Volume Dial accesses the selected tab's audio stream to control volume, apply sound effects, and display audio levels. It processes the stream in real time on your device. The extension does not record, store, or transmit tab audio. It does not access your microphone.

## Site presets and preferences

When you open the extension on a website, it reads the site's origin (scheme, host, and port) to look up a saved preset. If you save a site preset, the extension stores that origin together with the audio settings in Chrome's local extension storage. It uses the origin only to find the preset. It does not store page paths or keep a browsing history. Global audio presets and appearance preferences are also stored locally. These settings are not sent to Aimplify Solutions LLC or synced by the extension.

## Sharing and retention

The extension does not send user data to Aimplify Solutions LLC or other parties. It does not use analytics or advertising. Saved presets remain in the browser until you select **Forget** for the preset or remove the extension. You can reset appearance preferences in **Appearance**. The company website link opens a separate website when you choose to follow it.

Tab Volume Dial's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/policies#protecting_user_privacy), including the Limited Use requirements.
