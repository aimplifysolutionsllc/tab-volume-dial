TAB VOLUME DIAL
By Aimplify Solutions LLC | https://aimplifysolutions.com/

Install in Chrome 116 or later
1. Extract the ZIP to a folder you plan to keep.
2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
3. Click Load unpacked and select the Tab Volume Dial folder containing manifest.json.
4. Open Chrome's Extensions menu and pin Tab Volume Dial.

If you already loaded this unpacked folder, click Reload on its extension card
after updating the files.

Volume and comparison
Open the menu on the tab you want to adjust. Drag and release the slider or type
an exact percentage up to 1000% and press Enter. Use the step buttons for quick changes. Hear original restores native tab audio
for comparison. Restore settings reapplies your adjustments. Reset clears the
tab's adjustments. Active settings last until the tab closes or Chrome exits.

Listening modes and tone
Speech brings softer voices forward and cuts low rumble. Night uses stronger
compression to reduce jumps between dialogue and loud effects. Music starts
with flat EQ and preserves dynamics until peaks reach the limiter. Open
Equalizer & stereo for five EQ bands, left/right balance, mono, and an optional leveler.

Levels and site presets
The meters show the louder channel's RMS level before and after processing.
Peak protection limits loud peaks, so the actual boost can be lower than the
slider setting. Meters run while adjustments are active.
Use Presets to save settings locally for This site or All sites. Choose
Apply saved from the popup whenever you want to use them on a tab.

Appearance
Open the gear beside the title to choose Dark, Light, or Match system and an
accent color. These preferences are saved locally for all tabs.

The extension processes audio locally. It does not record, save, or transmit it.
Some browser pages restrict tab capture.
